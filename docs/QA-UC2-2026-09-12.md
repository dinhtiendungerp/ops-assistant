## 3. Loi logic trong lop AL, day la phat hien quan trong nhat

Ba loi ma `CLAUDE.md` liet ke o muc "loi da mac" so 5, 6, 7 da duoc sua trong lop Python
(`inventory.py` ghi ro "ba diem ban truoc lam sai") nhung **chua bao gio duoc sua trong AL**.
Hai phien truoc coi Python la duong chinh nen bo qua AL. Gio AL la duong chinh, ba loi do dang
nam trong app san pham vua build.

Mo phong dung logic cua `NWVInvHealthCalc.Codeunit.al` tren bo du lieu demo, neo 18/09/2026:

Chin dong xep sai tang. 10 tren 45 lo dang ton o kho W0003 bi AL xep "cham luan chuyen".

### 3.1 Nhu cau tai kho tinh bang dong Sale

`GetSalesVelocity` chi cong Entry Type = Sale. Kho W0003 xuat hang di cua hang bang Negative
Adjmt., khong co dong Sale nao, nen AvgDaily = 0, Days of Cover = 9999, Last Sale = 0D,
Days Since Last Sale = 91, roi roi vao Slow Moving. Day la loi so 6 trong brief.
Python sua: dia diem nao khong he co dong Sale thi lay tong luong xuat lam nhu cau.

### 3.2 Cau bi cat cut

AvgDaily = tong ban chia deu 90 ngay, khong loai ngay ton bang 0. Mat hang 33200 tai S0001 co
14 ngay het hang: AL ra 6,61 mot ngay, Python ra 7,75. Lech 17%. Nguong bo sung tinh tren con
so thap se lam dut hang tiep. Day la loi so 5, va la tinh huong ghim so 1 trong README-import,
duoc dung ra dung de bat loi nay.

### 3.3 Muc ton muc tieu khong bi chan theo han dung

`NWVReplenishmentCalc`: TargetQty = AvgDaily x Target Days of Cover, khong xet han dung.
De xuat bo sung du ban 14 ngay cho mat hang han dung 2 ngay. Loi so 7. Tren bo demo,
9 tren 22 de xuat phai bi chan.

### 3.4 Today() thay vi WorkDate()

Ca hai codeunit dung `Today()`. Bo demo neo o 18/09/2026 va README dan dat Work Date ngay do.
Work Date khong anh huong Today(). Chay demo ngay 15/09 thi moi con so "con bao nhieu ngay"
lech ba ngay so voi tai lieu. Doi sang `WorkDate()` la xong.

### 3.5 Nho hon

- Replenishment quet moi Location neu `Store Location Filter` trong: 32 location x 865 item.
  Setup cho demo phai dien W0003 va S0001|S0002|S0005|S0010|S0013.
- Diem Near Expiry: Python chan 65 khi du kien ban het truoc han, AL khong chan. Khong doi tang.
- Inventory Value = Quantity x Item."Unit Cost", tai lieu ghi ban chinh thuc dung Value Entry.
  Chap nhan cho POC, da ghi trong doc.
- Cua so 90 ngay: AL lay `CalcDate('<-90D>')` den Today la 91 ngay nhung chia 90. Python lay dung
  90 ngay. Lech nho, nen thong nhat.
